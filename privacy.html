HomeAgent privacy policy

HomeAgent is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, and it only accesses the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

Gmail messages and labels, calendar events (read only) and Google Tasks.

What it does with the data

It reads mail to sort it into labels, reads calendar events to apply the owner's rules, and creates tasks and labels. It never deletes, trashes or edits mail, events or tasks.

Where the data goes

Data is processed and stored on the owner's own home server. Mail content is not sent to third parties. The data is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
